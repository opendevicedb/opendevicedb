# Repository guidance

## Community research and outreach

For work researching communities or contributors, promoting OpenDeviceDB, or contacting people:

- Read `docs/outreach/README.md`, `docs/outreach/research.md` and `docs/outreach/log.json` before contacting anyone; check existing threads and sent history to avoid duplicates.
- Record public research sources and findings, including leads not contacted and why.
- After each authorized send or publication, archive our outgoing professional message in `docs/outreach/messages/` and update the contact log with its date, channel, evidence, actual status and next step.
- Keep sent, published, moderation-pending and substantive-response statuses distinct. Proposed integrations and imported support listings are not implemented work or hardware validation.
- This is a public repository. Keep personal correspondence, mailbox identifiers, raw mail headers, residence details, phone numbers and confirmation/cancellation tokens outside it.
