Subject: Tip: a maintained list of which Sony cameras can run custom firmware and apps

Hi Andrea,

Following your "hacking festival" roundup (https://www.sonyalpharumors.com/and-the-hacking-festival-is-continuing-a7iii-29min-limit-gone-a6000-gets-silent-shutter-and-sony-a1-file-transfer-app/), you may find this useful: we maintain an open, searchable list of the cameras that can run these projects, model by model.

Sony view: https://opendevicedb.org/cameras/?brand=Sony

It covers 129 Sony cameras: the PlayMemories bodies that take custom apps through Sony-PMCA-RE (OpenMemories Tweak, Recipe Lab), and the newer research projects (A7 IV, A6000, A6400, A1 II, FX30, A7C II). For each one it gives the project, the stock firmware the build needs, whether the author tested it on that body, the brick risk and the licence, with links to the repositories. Research projects are marked as such, with the boot-loop reports their authors published.

The full list across brands (Canon, Nikon, OM System/Olympus, Fujifilm…) is at https://opendevicedb.org/cameras/, filtered views can be linked, and the data is open (CC BY-SA) if you want to reuse it. Corrections are welcome.
